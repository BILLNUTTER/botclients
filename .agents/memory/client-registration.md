---
name: Client registration fields
description: Nutterx Connect client registration and service data requirements.
---

Registering a client requires only name, phone number, amount paid, service, and service expiry. Do not require or add service types to client registration or service entry.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested a short registration form without service types.

**How to apply:** keep these five inputs as the registration form contract; generate internal IDs and service start dates in the application.
